## Licensing result

Getty's [content license agreement](https://www.gettyimages.com/eula), section
3.11, requires express authorization in its invoice/order/license for machine-learning
or AI purposes. Its definition expressly includes evaluation. Buying an ordinary
editorial download is therefore not sufficient for this benchmark. Both Syndics
product pages also require contacting the local office for commercial use.

Alamy's [UK and rest-of-world agreement](https://www.alamy.com/terms/uk/), section
2.1, limits use to the purposes specified in the invoice. It does not give an explicit
benchmark permission in the public text reviewed. Its preview clause permits internal
review, which must not be confused with a paid license to add previews to the test
set. Ask for the specific evaluation use to be stated on the invoice. This is a
procurement requirement for our intended use, not a claim that Alamy prohibits all
AI evaluation.

The PHAS/Getty Austrian product page displayed EUR 335 for a 1024 × 711 download
when checked. That is an ordinary product-page price, **not** an AI-evaluation quote,
not a Netherlands checkout price and not a reliable budget for the nine-image set.
No usable total price or confirmed evaluation license was obtained. Avoid purchasing
a subscription or multiplying this displayed rate into a proposed project budget.
