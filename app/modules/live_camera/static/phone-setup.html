VisitAdvisorLOCAL IPHONE TEST

ONE-TIME SETUP

Open your camera.
Keep it local.

Connect your iPhone to the same Wi-Fi as the computer running VisitAdvisor. Safari requires a trusted HTTPS connection for camera access.

  1. Download the local test certificate and allow the download.

  2. Open Settings → Profile Downloaded and install VisitAdvisor local camera trial.

  3. Open Settings → General → About → Certificate Trust Settings. Enable full trust for VisitAdvisor local camera trial.

  4. Open the live camera prototype, tap Start live camera, and allow camera access.

This certificate is generated on your computer for this seven-day test. It adds a trusted root on your phone. Remove the profile from Settings → General → VPN & Device Management after testing.

Camera imagery stays on your phone. The model download is about 84 MB plus the browser runtime. The first demo supports The Milkmaid and The Jewish Bride.
